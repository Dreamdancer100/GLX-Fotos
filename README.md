## ✨ One program instead of five

Photo cleanup, image conversion, background removal, AI enlargement and enhancement, image editing, video trimming, PDF editing, printing and scanning — GLX Fotos brings these everyday tasks together in one Windows application.

Your photos and documents stay on your computer. You do not have to upload private images to websites just to process them.

## 🧹 Find duplicate photos by image content

GLX Fotos compares the actual image content, not just filenames. It can find duplicates even when the same photo exists as an iPhone HEIC file and a JPEG, or as an original and a smaller WhatsApp copy.

- Select one folder or several folders, network drives and external drives.
- Review each group with previews, see which file is suggested to keep and why, and see how much storage could be reclaimed.
- **Identical** means byte-for-byte identical. **Similar** means the same image in a different size or quality; these groups are labelled separately.
- The suggested file to keep is your edited version, when available. Otherwise GLX Fotos prefers the oldest capture date, then the highest resolution. You can change the suggestion with one click.
- Damaged and partially transferred images are listed on a separate tab. They are never automatically suggested for deletion.

### Nothing is removed without your confirmation

- Files are removed only after you confirm, and are sent to the Windows Recycle Bin — including files on USB drives and external disks.
- On network drives, files are moved to a folder named **_GLX-Fotos-entfernt** next to your pictures.
- Editing tools never overwrite the original. Converted, cut-out, enlarged and cropped images are always saved as new files.

## 📱 iPhone and Android

- Convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPEG or PNG while preserving the capture date.
- GLX Fotos recognises Live Photos. The accompanying short video is never treated as a duplicate photo for deletion.
- Connect an iPhone or Android phone by cable and import photos.
- Files already on your PC are skipped. Nothing is changed or deleted on the phone.

## ✂️ AI background removal

Remove a subject from its background with one click — people, animals, plants, objects and logos on plain backgrounds.

- Click the background to remove the area up to its edges; enclosed areas can optionally be included.
- Refine the result by clicking, with a brush or rectangle, or by removing a selected colour.
- Adjust strength and edge softness, and inspect details with the magnifier.
- Process many images in one batch.
- Save as PNG with a transparent background or directly as an ICO icon.
- Place the cut-out subject on a new background. Move it, resize it, and adjust opacity, brightness, colour and outline.

## 🔎 Enlarge images with AI

Choose quick, classic enlargement for results in seconds, or use an AI model that can recover details in hair, lettering and edges more accurately.

The preview uses the available workspace. Use **Fit** to see the complete image at any time.

## ✨ Improve images with one click

Click **Enhance** and GLX Fotos checks the image for issues such as:

- Flat contrast, underexposure or overexposure
- Colour casts and faded colours
- Blur and noise
- Compression blocks and pixelation
- Images that are too small
- Red eyes

Switch each suggested correction on or off and adjust its strength. A split line in the preview compares before and after.

AI processing for small or pixelated images runs on your PC. Good photos are left alone: strong colours and dark images are not automatically washed out. When there is nothing useful to improve, GLX Fotos says so.

## 🎨 Image editor — familiar tools, clearly organised

The editor offers menus for **File, Edit, View, Image and Tools**, with labelled buttons. Tools are listed on the left; the settings panel below shows only the options relevant to the selected tool.

- Rectangular and freehand selection, crop, copy and paste
- Pencil, brush, spray, eraser, fill with tolerance and colour picker
- Text with font, size, bold and italic
- Shapes including stars, arrows, speech bubbles and hearts
- Resize while keeping the aspect ratio, rotate, mirror and crop
- Grid, rulers and multiple undo steps
- Create a new image by pixel dimensions or as A5, A4 or A3 paper, portrait or landscape, at 150, 300 or 600 dpi
- Save as PNG, JPEG or WebP. Transparent PNG backgrounds remain transparent.

## 🧩 Windows icons and Store graphics

Turn an image into a Windows ICO file containing sizes from 16 to 256 pixels.

You can also create Store graphics as PNG files in 71, 150 and 300 pixel sizes. Rectangular images are never distorted. Small images can be enlarged with AI before export.

## 📄 PDFs, documents, printing and scanning

Open PDFs, images, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files. Multi-page PDFs appear with all pages in the sidebar.

- Reorder, rotate or delete pages, insert blank pages and combine documents.
- Place an image or a PDF page onto another page — for example, a photo on an A4 page.
- Add text, highlights, drawings, rectangles, circles, lines, arrows, cover areas and signatures to pages.
- Select, move, resize, recolour and delete added elements. Lines snap to 0°, 45° and 90°; alignment guides help position objects.
- Copy a selected area as an image to the clipboard.
- Save as PDF or image.
- Print through the Windows print dialog. GLX Fotos can use its own default printer setting without changing the Windows default printer.
- Scan from a flatbed or document feeder, page by page or continuously, with paper size and progress display. Many network scanners work without installing a separate driver.
- Word documents retain their original layout when Microsoft Office or LibreOffice is installed. Otherwise, they are displayed in a simplified layout.

## 🎬 Trim videos

Trim the beginning and end of a video. While dragging the trim handles, you can preview the exact frame at that position.

Adjust the volume or mute the audio. MP4 and MOV videos are cut in seconds without re-encoding or loss of quality. The original file remains untouched.

## 🧰 Everyday convenience

- Open files by double-clicking when you choose GLX Fotos as the Windows default app: images open in the editor, PDFs and documents in **Print & Documents**, and videos in the video tool.
- If GLX Fotos is already open, the file is sent to the existing window.
- GLX Fotos never changes Windows default apps on its own.
- Choose from 12 themes: GLX Neon, Dark, Light, Black, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Ruby, Sand, Lavender, Silver Shell and High Contrast.
- German and English interface languages can be switched without restarting.
- Built-in help with topics and search, available offline.
- One-click updates.

## 🔒 Your data stays on your PC

Photos, folder lists and usage statistics are not uploaded. AI processing runs locally on your computer. The only external connection is the licence check.
